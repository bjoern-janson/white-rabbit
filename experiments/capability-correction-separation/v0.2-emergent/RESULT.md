## Result boundary

This run establishes a **synthetic local specimen** in which an ordinary current-workload optimizer, without access to the future defeater, selected a more productive accumulated state whose later minimum validated correction cost was higher than that of the otherwise matched history with a generic preservation constraint.

It does not establish that real AI systems naturally acquire corrective debt, that compression generally raises correction cost, or that any proposed mechanism generalizes.
