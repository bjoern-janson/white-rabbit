# Formal Confirmation

The formal lane is intentionally frozen while White Rabbit is hunting for the phenomenon.

Nothing in this file authorizes model execution.

## Why it exists

Exploration finds interesting discontinuities. Formal confirmation checks whether they survive controlled reproduction and whether the proposed explanation is actually supported.

```text
hunt -> explain -> confirm
```

Do not reverse that order.
