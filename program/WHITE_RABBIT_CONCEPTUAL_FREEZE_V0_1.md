## Economic hierarchy

The frozen hierarchy is:

```text
Optimization
    <
Compilation
    <
Amortization
    <
Compounding
```

Here `<` means increasing empirical burden. It does not mean mathematical subset or logical entailment.

### Optimization

Make the present computation cheaper.

### Compilation

Replace repeated computation with reusable structure `M`.

### Amortization

Repay acquisition, realization, monitoring, revision, and use costs over the constituted reuse horizon.

### Compounding

Make the improvement process more economically effective at producing useful future reusable structures.

Minimum White Rabbit success ends at `AMORTIZATION`. Compounding is a stronger future result and is not mandatory for a White Rabbit.
